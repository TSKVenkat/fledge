# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a project used by teachers and children; behave as you would
in their classroom.

## In practice

Expected: courtesy, patience with beginners, and criticism aimed at the code
rather than the person who wrote it.

Not accepted: harassment of any kind, personal attacks, publishing someone's
private information, sexualised language or imagery, or sustained disruption.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, in pull requests, in commit messages, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers by opening a confidential security advisory
on the repository, or by contacting a maintainer listed in CODEOWNERS directly.
Reports are handled privately. Maintainers who do not uphold this document in
good faith may be removed from the project.
